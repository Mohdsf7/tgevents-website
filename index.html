How it works.

You share your guest list. We take care of every message, reply and arrival, and you follow along on a live dashboard.

  1. 1

    The invitation

    Each guest receives a personal WhatsApp invitation with your names, date and venue, and two buttons: attending or not.

  2. 2

    Replies & reminders

    Replies are recorded instantly, in Arabic or English. Guests who haven't answered get a gentle reminder; your family can be kept updated.

  3. 3

    A full house

    When a guest declines, the next person on your backup list is invited automatically, within your capacity.

  4. 4

    Pass & preferences

    Confirmed guests receive their personal entry pass, and can tell us about allergies or dietary needs in a short form.

  5. 5

    The arrival

    At the door, each pass is scanned in a second. Copied or reused passes are refused, and VIP areas only open for VIP guests.

  6. 6

    After the event

    You receive a clear summary in Arabic and English: who came, replies, backups called in and how the evening went at the door.

Questions hosts ask.

Do our guests need to download an app?

No. Invitations and replies happen on WhatsApp, and passes open in the phone's browser. Guests simply show their pass, or its QR code, at the door.

Can guests reply in Arabic?

Yes. Guests can tap a button or write their answer in Arabic or English; we understand both and reply in the guest's language.

What happens when a guest declines?

If you keep a backup list, the next guest is invited automatically, as long as their party fits within your capacity. You can follow every change on your dashboard.

How early should we start?

We recommend six to eight weeks before the event, so guests have time to reply and backups can be invited comfortably.

Can we send our own video or design?

Yes. Invitations can carry your names, date and venue, and can include a video invitation. We'll help you prepare the wording in both languages.

What if a guest arrives without their pass?

With Full service, our team at the door can find the guest on the list and welcome them personally.

Do you work outside Dubai?

We work with weddings and private events across the United Arab Emirates. Contact us for events elsewhere in the region.

Data deletion

You may request deletion of personal information connected to The Gate Events and its guest-management services.

How to request deletion

Email info@tgevents.ae with the subject “Data deletion request”. Include the name, mobile number and event needed to identify the relevant record.

Do not send identity documents unless we specifically request them through an appropriate channel. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting.

What happens next

We will delete or anonymise eligible information and confirm the outcome, except where retention is required for legal, security, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.
